# audio-convert

The `istar-audio-converter` binary: audio conversion from Dolphin's context menu. What it does, its options and its
quality settings are on the wiki page [Audio converter](https://github.com/Istarlabs/istar-media-converter/wiki/Audio-converter).

A Rust port of [dolphin-audio-converter](https://github.com/SavageCore/dolphin-audio-converter) by SavageCore. This
crate stays under that project's MIT licence ([LICENSE](LICENSE)); the rest of the workspace is GPL-3.0-or-later.
